Frequently Asked Questions

A Few Common Questions

Please bring previous medical reports, scans, test results, a list of your current medicines and any relevant allergy information. You may also note down questions you would like to discuss.

Do not stop or change prescribed medicines unless your doctor tells you to. If you have been asked to prepare for a test or procedure, confirm any medication instructions with your care team.

Fasting is not required for every appointment or test. Follow the specific instructions provided by your hospital team and contact them if you are unsure.
