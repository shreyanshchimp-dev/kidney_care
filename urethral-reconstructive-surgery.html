Understanding

What are
Urethral Conditions?

The urethra is the tube that carries urine from the bladder out of the body. Urethral problems can occur due to narrowing (stricture), injury, infection or congenital conditions. These conditions can affect urine flow and may cause discomfort or difficulty in urination. A proper evaluation helps identify the exact cause and determine the most appropriate treatment, which may include reconstructive surgery in some cases.

Common Symptoms

Urethral conditions may cause a range of urinary symptoms, including:

  • Weak or slow urine flow
  • Urine spraying or split stream
  • Difficulty starting urination
  • Burning or pain during urination
  • Urine spraying or split stream
  • Blood in the urine (in some cases)
  • Feeling of incomplete bladder emptying
  • Blood in the urine (in some cases)
