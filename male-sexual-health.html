Understanding
Male Sexual Health
& Infertility

Male sexual health includes sexual performance, erectile function, and overall reproductive health. Male infertility refers to the inability to achieve pregnancy despite regular, unprotected intercourse.

These conditions can have physical, hormonal, psychological or lifestyle-related causes. A proper evaluation helps identify the cause and determine the most suitable treatment.

Common Symptoms

Symptoms can vary depending on the underlying cause. Common symptoms include:

  • Difficulty in getting or maintaining erection
  • Difficulty in conceiving a child
  • Reduced sexual desire
  • Testicular pain or swelling
  • Premature ejaculation
  • Hormonal imbalance symptoms
  • Low sperm count or poor sperm quality
  • Reduced sexual satisfaction
