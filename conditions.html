Common Urological Conditions

Kidney & Urinary Tract Conditions

  • Kidney stones

Prostate Conditions

  • Enlarged prostate (BPH)
  • Prostatitis

  • Prostate-related urinary symptoms

Bladder Conditions

  • Bladder stones
  • Urinary tract infections (UTI)
  • Blood in the urine (Hematuria)
  • Urinary incontinence

Urethral & Urinary Flow Problems

  • Urethral stricture
  • Difficulty passing urine
  • Weak or interrupted flow
  • Urinary retention

Male Sexual & Reproductive Health

  • Erectile dysfunction (ED)
  • Premature ejaculation
  • Male infertility
  • Low sperm count

Women’s & Children’s Urological Conditions

  • Urinary tract infections
  • Urinary incontinence (women)
  • Bedwetting (children)
  • Voiding problems
  • Other childhood urinary concerns
